3. Cookies, Consent Storage & Embedded Media

This website does not use analytics or advertising trackers. It stores only your Vimeo privacy choice in your browser’s local storage under the key yi-vimeo-consent. This is necessary to remember whether Vimeo content may be loaded and remains stored until you change the setting or clear your browser data.

Vimeo players are blocked by default. They are loaded only after you select “Allow Vimeo videos” or activate a Vimeo placeholder. Once allowed, your browser connects directly to Vimeo.com, Inc., 330 West 34th Street, 5th Floor, New York, New York 10001, USA. Vimeo may process your IP address, browser and device information, referrer data, playback interactions and information stored in or read from your device.

The legal basis for loading Vimeo and the associated data processing is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. Vimeo may process information in the United States and other countries. The players use Vimeo’s Do Not Track option, but this does not prevent the technical connection to Vimeo. You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. Withdrawing consent prevents new Vimeo players from loading but cannot reverse processing that already occurred.

Further information is available in Vimeo’s Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.
