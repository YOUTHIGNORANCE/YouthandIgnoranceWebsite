2. Technical Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted externally via GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you visit this website, GitHub's servers automatically save standard delivery logs. This includes your IP address, browser type, operating system, referral URL, and the time of your request. This data collection is strictly necessary to transmit the website to your monitor securely and reliably. The legal basis for this processing is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in operating a secure website).

GitHub processes this data partially in the United States. Data transfer to the US is safeguarded by GitHub’s active certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

3. Cookies, Tracking & Embedded Media

This website does not use its own analytics, advertising trackers, or first-party cookies.

Some project pages automatically load embedded videos from Vimeo.com, Inc. When a Vimeo player is loaded, your browser connects directly to Vimeo. Vimeo may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device information, referrer data, and playback interactions. The player is configured with Vimeo’s Do Not Track option, which limits session tracking and non-essential cookies; essential security cookies may still be used, and cookies already stored by Vimeo may be transmitted by your browser.

Further information is available in Vimeo’s Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

4. Contacting Us via E-Mail

If you contact us via our provided e-mail address, your e-mail, its metadata, and the personal data contained within it will be stored by us for the sole purpose of processing your inquiry. We do not pass this data on without your consent. The legal basis for processing this data is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual relations) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

5. Your Rights as a User

Under the GDPR, you have the right to request information about your stored personal data, its origin, recipients, and the purpose of its collection at no charge. You also have the right to request that it be corrected, blocked, or deleted. To exercise these rights, simply email us.

Furthermore, you have the right to lodge a formal complaint with the competent supervisory authority. For our studio, this is the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Baden-Württemberg (LfDI Baden-Württemberg).
